# Time Management, Habit Building, and Focus

## 1. Overcoming Academic Procrastination
- **The 5-Minute Rule**: The hardest part of studying is initiating the session (activation energy). Tell yourself: *"I will sit down and do just 5 minutes of reading/math."* In over 80% of cases, the brain enters flow state and continues past 5 minutes.
- **Micro-Chunking Tasks**:
  - Instead of *"Study Organic Chemistry"*, break it down to: *"Read pages 42-47 and draw reaction mechanism #1"*.
  - Specific, low-friction micro-tasks reduce cognitive resistance.

## 2. The Student Eisenhower Matrix
- **Quadrant 1 (Urgent & Important)**: Imminent assignment deadlines, exams tomorrow. (Do now).
- **Quadrant 2 (Not Urgent, but Important)**: Long-term revision, project drafting, sleep, exercise. (Schedule dedicated daily blocks; this prevents Quadrant 1 crises).
- **Quadrant 3 (Urgent, Not Important)**: Group chat pings, non-essential calls. (Batch or delegate).
- **Quadrant 4 (Neither Urgent nor Important)**: Infinite social media scrolling, binge-watching when stressed. (Limit consciously).

## 3. Daily Study Routines & Habit Stacking
- **Habit Stacking Formula**: *"After [Current Habit], I will [New Study Habit]"*.
  - Example: *"After I finish my morning coffee, I will write today's Top 3 priorities on a sticky note."*
- **The Power of Study Environments**:
  - Dedicate a specific desk or library corner exclusively for study. Avoid studying in bed to prevent associating your sleep sanctuary with stress.
