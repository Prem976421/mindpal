5-4-3-2-1 Somatic Grounding

Pulls your attention out of anxious spirals and anchors you back into physical reality.

👀 5 things you can SEE: Look around your desk/room. Notice a pencil, a shadow, a book spine.
✋ 4 things you can TOUCH: Feel the texture of your desk, your shirt, or your feet on the floor.
👂 3 things you can HEAR: Listen closely. Fan hum, distant voices, or your own breath.
👃 2 things you can SMELL: Your coffee, paper, or the air in the room.
💬 1 positive truth: Tell yourself: "This anxiety is uncomfortable, but it is temporary and I am safe."
